FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Call us on 067 723 6716.

How long does a bespoke suit take?

Typically 2–4 weeks depending on fabric availability and fitting schedule. Express options are available.

Do you take walk-ins or do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for alterations. Bespoke and bridal consultations are by appointment for an unhurried experience.

Can I bring my own fabric?

Absolutely. We also keep a curated library of premium wool, cotton, silk and traditional textiles.

Do you handle bulk corporate uniform orders?

Yes. We tailor uniforms for hospitality, corporate and school clients with volume pricing and on-site fittings.

What areas do you service?

Cosmo City, Randburg, Roodepoort and greater Johannesburg. Pickup & delivery available on request.
